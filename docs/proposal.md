# **Pengajuan Proposal TA di iGracias**


## **Pengajuan Proposal**
1. Login ke [iGracias](https://igracias.telkomuniversity.ac.id/)
  ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/s1trm/knowledge/refs/heads/master/gambar/proposal/proposal1.png)

1. Pilih menu **TA/PA --> Daftar TA/PA --> Status TA/PA Mahasiswa**. 
  ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/s1trm/knowledge/refs/heads/master/gambar/proposal/proposal2.png)

1. Pilih **Pengajuan Proposal**. 
  ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/s1trm/knowledge/refs/heads/master/gambar/proposal/proposal3.png)

1. Upload proposal dan juga isi form proposal. Untuk **Kelompok Keahlian** isi dengan **Applied Digital Business, Entrepreneurships and Tourism**.
  ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/s1trm/knowledge/refs/heads/master/gambar/proposal/proposal4.png)
  ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/s1trm/knowledge/refs/heads/master/gambar/proposal/proposal5.png)
  ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/s1trm/knowledge/refs/heads/master/gambar/proposal/proposal6.png)
  ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/s1trm/knowledge/refs/heads/master/gambar/proposal/proposal7.png)
  ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/s1trm/knowledge/refs/heads/master/gambar/proposal/proposal8.png)

1. Setelah selesai, tekan **Simpan**.

## **Pengajuan Pembimbing**
1. Pilih menu **Pengajuan Pembimbing**. Lalu pilih Pembimbing 1 dan Pembimbing 2 sesuai plotting yang dilakukan koordinator PA.
  ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/s1trm/knowledge/refs/heads/master/gambar/proposal/proposal9.png)
